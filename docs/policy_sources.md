**NCDC surveillance definition.** A confirmed heatstroke death requires several criteria together: ante-mortem core temperature, cooling attempted, altered mental status, no other diagnosis, and an environmental or heat-stress history. Anything else is "suspected". Reported via IHIP under NPCCHH.
- [Checklist, Oct 2025](https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Checklist_Criteria-to-Categorize-Heat-Stroke-or-HRI-Deaths-in-Clinical-Setting.pdf)
- [FAQ 2024](https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/FAQ-on-Heat-Related-Illness-Death-Surveillance_2024.pdf)

The 2025 surveillance count reportedly had 14 confirmed deaths (1 Mar–24 Jun) against 7,192 suspected cases ([The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/climate-change/india-heatwave-heatstroke-deaths-mortality-data)), grade S.

## Why this matters for the paper

1. **Mechanism hypothesis.** Compensation creates a certification pathway: a doctor certifies, the Tahsildar is informed, and money is paid. Death is then more likely to be classified and recorded as heat or sunstroke. The three highest exposure-adjusted recorders (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Punjab) and Odisha include the states with the longest-standing payment schemes (undivided AP since 2011; Odisha SDRF).
   - This is ecological and confounded. Present it as a hypothesis consistent with the index, not as an effect estimate.
   - Falsification check, first pass: Punjab, the highest index (4.5–4.9), has no heat-specific scheme found, and Bihar's payment was ad hoc in 2019.
   - So compensation is at most part of the story. Other candidates are police inquest practice (the ADSI unit is a police record), media salience, and district administration practice.
   - Do not frame the paper around compensation.
2. **Live policy hook.** National notification for 2026–31 makes ₹4 lakh ex-gratia available nationwide.
   - Recording will change because payment now depends on certification, so post-2026 counts are not comparable with earlier ones.
   - This paper gives the exposure-adjusted pre-notification baseline by state.
   - Any SDRF allocation or Heat Action Plan evaluation that uses recorded heat deaths will reward recording, not risk.
3. **Natural experiments for later.** Telangana (Apr 2025, ₹50k → ₹4 lakh) and Tamil Nadu (Oct 2024) can be tested once ADSI 2025 is released.
